// 01  MINDSET

Job hunting is a systems problem, not a motivation problem.

Telling people to "apply more" ignores the math. The bottleneck is throughput and follow-through — exactly the kind of problem you solve with engineering, not willpower. I built the system I wished existed when I was on the other side of the table.

// 02  THE BIGGER PROBLEM

Hiring is automated on one side only.

Employers screen with ATS filters, keyword matching, and AI. Candidates still copy-paste the same details into hundreds of forms by hand, get ghosted, and never reach a real person. Good people lose not because they're unqualified but because the process is stacked against them.

// 03  THE SOLUTION

Give candidates the same automation advantage employers already have.

Novrant AI runs eight specialised agents in a pipeline: discovery across LinkedIn and 20+ boards, fit scoring against the candidate's profile, application tailoring, form submission through browser automation, and direct outreach to hiring contacts with MX-validated email addresses — so messages land in real inboxes, not the void.

DISCOVERSCORETAILORAPPLYOUTREACHTRACK
  • Built with Python, Playwright, FastAPI and LLM agents — no off-the-shelf wrapper.
  • Every application is logged with a full audit trail; regulated-systems habits applied to a consumer product.
  • Internal ops platform for monitoring runs, quality and customer accounts.
  • Three tiers — Starter, Pro, Elite — priced by volume and outreach depth.
